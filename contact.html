❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I submit my own prompt templates to be featured on PromptMastery?

Absolutely! We love seeing what our community creates. If you've developed a prompt that consistently produces great results, send it our way via email. Include the prompt text, which AI model you tested it on, and a brief description of the results you've seen. If it meets our quality standards after testing, we'll feature it with full credit to you. Some of our most popular prompts have come from reader contributions.

Do you offer one-on-one prompt engineering consulting?

Currently, all of our resources are provided through our free written guides. We don't offer paid one-on-one consulting at this time. However, our guides are designed to be comprehensive enough to address most prompting challenges — check our main cheat sheet for foundational techniques, or our specialized guides for business, content creation, and productivity use cases. If you have a specific question, feel free to email us and I'll do my best to point you in the right direction.

How often is the content on PromptMastery updated?

We review and update all of our guides on a monthly basis. AI models change frequently — a new model version can alter how prompts perform — so we continuously test and refine our recommendations. Every page displays a "Last Updated" date so you can see exactly how current the information is. If you notice something outdated between updates, please let us know and we'll prioritize a revision.

I'm interested in a partnership or guest post. What's the process?

We're selectively open to partnerships that align with our mission of making AI more accessible. If you represent an AI tool, educational platform, or technology publication and would like to collaborate, send us an email with details about your organization and your proposal. We evaluate all partnership inquiries based on relevance to our audience, quality standards, and alignment with our editorial values. Please allow 3–5 business days for a response on partnership inquiries.

Can I use the prompts from PromptMastery for commercial purposes?

Yes! All prompt templates published on PromptMastery are free to use for both personal and commercial purposes. You're welcome to adapt them for your business, client work, or projects. We only ask that you don't republish our guides themselves (the written content and analysis) without permission. The prompts are tools — use them however serves you best.
